Privacy

Galley Chaos · last updated 16 November 2026

If you just play

When a flight ends, the game sends an anonymous record: aircraft, round length, score, result, flight time and a random ID created in your browser. The ID is not linked to your name, email or device details. It is used only to count how many people play.

If you sign in with Google (optional)

The game receives your name, email address and profile picture link from Google and stores your name and email with your flight records, so your scores belong to your account. Nothing is posted to your Google account and your contacts are never accessed.

Who can see it

Only the game's maintainer, Kunal Bhatia, through a private admin page. The data is stored with Supabase in Mumbai, India. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

Your choices

  • Play without signing in. The game works fully.
  • Sign out at any time from the start screen.
  • To have your account and records deleted, open an issue at github.com/kunalmbhatia/galley-chaos or message the maintainer there.

On your device

Progress, sound setting and the random ID are kept in your browser's local storage. Clearing site data removes them. The game uses no advertising cookies and no third-party trackers.
