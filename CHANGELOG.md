# Galley Chaos release notes

## v2.1 "Smooth Take-off" · 17 November 2026

Early players told us the first flight ended in a Cabin Meltdown before they worked out what to click. The numbers agreed: 12 of the first 13 flights ended in a meltdown. This update fixes the first flight and makes each aircraft a gentler step up.

**Easier start**
- The ATR 72 now has a first-flight coach: a pointer shows which passenger to click, and the right cart item flashes until you land your first flight.
- Only one passenger stands up during the ATR climb (it was four), and they wait 22 seconds instead of 10.
- Passengers on the ATR wait about twice as long, and requests arrive more slowly.
- A missed passenger on the ATR adds 5% chaos instead of 12%.

**Difficulty now builds aircraft by aircraft**
- Chaos per missed passenger rises from 5% on the ATR 72 to 12% on the 777-300ER.
- Falling luggage rises from 6% to 15% chaos, and passengers out of their seat wait from 22 s down to 10 s.
- Each flight phase starts quietly and speeds up, instead of starting at full pace.

**Also in this version**
- How to play screen before the first flight, and from the start screen any time.
- Star rating after a flight, with quick reasons for low ratings.
